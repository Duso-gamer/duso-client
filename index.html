Duso Client

A free, community-made launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition. Sign in with your Microsoft account, manage your installations and mods, and customise your skin and cape – all in one place. The project is in active development.

Not an official Minecraft product. Duso Client is not approved by or associated with Mojang, Microsoft or Xbox. “Minecraft” is a trademark of Mojang AB. You need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition to play online.

What it does

  • Installations: create separate game installations with Vanilla, Fabric, Forge or NeoForge.
  • Mods, shaders, resource packs and data packs: search and install them from Modrinth (public API), including the extra mods they need.
  • Skins and capes: a skin editor, a cape editor and an optional community cape shop.
  • Settings: memory, graphics presets for more FPS, volume, key bindings, window size.
  • Log viewer and error helper: explains typical start-up errors and offers fixes.

Sign-in

Duso Client signs you in only with your Microsoft account, using the official Microsoft sign-in window and the standard Xbox / Minecraft services flow – the same flow every third-party launcher uses. The launcher never sees your password. Sign-in tokens stay on your own computer.

Duso Client does not bypass or weaken any licence or ownership check. Without a Minecraft: Java Edition licence on the signed-in account, the game only starts in demo mode.
